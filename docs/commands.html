Commands.

Every slash command Keking answers to. Type one in any channel the bot can read. Music playback is temporarily disabled; /join, /leave, and /tts remain available. AI chat is not a slash command: mention Keking or reply to one of its messages.

Music

/join

Use/join

/leave

DoesDisconnect Keking from the voice channel.
Use/leave

Music playback temporarily disabled

StatusOnly /join, /leave, and /tts are active. Set MUSIC_ENABLED=true and redeploy commands to restore playback commands.

Text-to-Speech

/tts

DoesSpeak text aloud in your voice channel. Uses ElevenLabs when configured; otherwise Google Translate TTS.
Use/tts good morning everyone

AI Chat

Mention / Reply

DoesChat with Keqing, the Yuheng of the Liyue Qixing. No slash command is used.
UseMention @Keking with a message, or reply to any message Keking created.
Reply contextIf you reply to a message, that referenced message is included as quoted context for the reply.
Channel recapAsk casually by mention or reply: “summarize this conversation”, “what happened in this channel?”, or “ringkas percakapan ini”. Up to 50 recent messages are used for that reply only.
VoiceIf Keking is in a voice channel (for example after /join or /tts), it speaks the reply with TTS.
